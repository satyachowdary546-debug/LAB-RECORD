Sports & Fitness

Stay active, develop teamwork and discover your potential through sports, fitness and recreational activities on campus.

Home   /   Sports

An Active Campus Community

Sports are an important part of student life. They provide opportunities to stay healthy, build confidence and develop teamwork and leadership skills.

Students can participate in individual and team sports, recreational activities, fitness programmes and campus competitions.

Whether you are an experienced athlete or simply looking for an enjoyable way to stay active, campus sports provide opportunities for students with different interests and abilities.

Sports Activities

Students can take part in a variety of sporting and recreational activities.

Cricket

A popular team sport offering students opportunities for practice, friendly matches and competitions.

Football

Students can participate in football training, recreational games and inter-class or campus competitions.

Basketball

Basketball activities provide students with opportunities to develop fitness, coordination and teamwork.

Badminton

Indoor badminton activities are suitable for recreational play, training and student competitions.

Athletics

Running and athletics activities help students improve endurance, speed and overall physical fitness.

Table Tennis

Table tennis provides an indoor recreational and competitive activity for students.

Student Participation

Sports provide students with opportunities to participate at different levels.

Getting Started

  • Choose a sport or recreational activity that interests you.
  • Check available practice sessions and facility schedules.
  • Participate in training and recreational activities.
  • Join student sports groups and competitions.
  • Follow the instructions of coaches and sports coordinators.

Sportsmanship

  • Respect teammates, opponents and officials.
  • Follow sports facility rules.
  • Use sports equipment responsibly.
  • Maintain appropriate sports attire and safety practices.
  • Promote teamwork, discipline and fair play.
