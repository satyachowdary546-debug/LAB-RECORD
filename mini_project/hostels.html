Comfortable Campus Living

University hostel life provides students with an opportunity to live close to their academic environment while becoming part of a vibrant student community.

The residential facilities are designed to provide students with accommodation, dining, study spaces, recreational areas and essential support services.

Students are encouraged to maintain a respectful and friendly environment while participating in academic, cultural and recreational activities.

Hostel Guidelines

Students are expected to follow residential rules to maintain a safe and respectful campus.

General Rules

  • Maintain cleanliness in rooms and common areas.
  • Respect fellow residents and hostel staff.
  • Take care of hostel property and shared facilities.
  • Follow university residential policies.
  • Report maintenance or safety concerns to hostel administration.

Student Responsibilities

  • Carry the required student identification.
  • Follow designated entry and exit procedures.
  • Maintain a peaceful study environment.
  • Follow dining and common-area guidelines.
  • Cooperate with hostel authorities during emergencies.
