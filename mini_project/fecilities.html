Facility Guidelines

Students are encouraged to use university facilities responsibly.

  • Maintain cleanliness in all campus facilities.
  • Handle university equipment and resources carefully.
  • Follow the rules and instructions applicable to each facility.
  • Respect other students, faculty and staff while using shared facilities.
  • Report damaged equipment or facility-related problems to the concerned department.
  • Follow safety instructions in laboratories, classrooms, sports areas and other facilities.
  • Use university resources for their intended academic or student-support purposes.
