Routes & Safety

Follow the transportation guidelines to make your journey safe and comfortable.

📍 Route Information

  • Check the correct bus route before travelling.
  • Use the designated pickup and drop-off points.
  • Arrive at the pickup location before departure.
  • Follow instructions given by transportation staff.
  • Check updated schedules when necessary.

🛡️ Travel Safety

  • Follow all university transportation rules.
  • Remain seated while the vehicle is moving.
  • Do not disturb the driver.
  • Keep personal belongings secure.
  • Maintain cleanliness inside the vehicle.

Student Travel Tips

Simple practices for a safe and comfortable transportation experience.

⏰ Be On Time

Reach your pickup point early so you do not miss your scheduled transportation.

🎒 Secure Belongings

Keep your bags, books, mobile phones and other personal belongings safe.

🛡️ Travel Safely

Follow safety instructions and behave responsibly while travelling.

❓ Ask for Help

Contact the concerned university staff if you have any transportation-related problem.
